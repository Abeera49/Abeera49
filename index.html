Industrial Training Kit

Train technicians who build it right the first time

Eight hands-on modules, from shop-floor safety to quality inspection, designed for your first ninety days on the line.

Explore the modules

Training modules

Each module combines a short briefing, a supervised practical and a sign-off checklist.

Shop-floor safety

PPE, lockout/tagout and emergency routes. 6 hours.

Welding basics

MIG and TIG fundamentals on mild steel. 12 hours.

CNC machining

Reading G-code, tool offsets and first-part setup. 16 hours.

Electrical systems

Schematics, three-phase supply and safe isolation. 10 hours.

Hydraulics

Pumps, valves and pressure testing. 8 hours.

Quality inspection

Calipers, micrometers and tolerance stacks. 8 hours.

Robotics cells

Cobot programming and safe cell access. 14 hours.

Preventive maintenance

Inspection rounds, lubrication and fault logging. 8 hours.

How the kit works

  1. Brief

    A 20-minute briefing sets the goal and the risks.

  2. Practice

    You work on a real rig with a supervisor beside you.

  3. Sign-off

    A checklist confirms the skill before you move on.
